## What's new

- **HBC Reborn letterheads.** Five new letterheads in a new HBC Reborn group: Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Night Sky and Arcade. Ocean, Night Sky and Arcade play a tune.

## Install

Extract the zip for your console to the root of your SD card (Wii also: or USB drive) and open PictoCard from the Homebrew Channel (Wii), the Wii U Menu (Wii U, needs Aroma), the Homebrew Menu (Switch) or the Homebrew Launcher (3DS).
