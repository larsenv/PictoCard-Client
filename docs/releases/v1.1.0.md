## What's new

- **Send from your own Wii.** Letters can now go out from your Wii's own Wii Number through WiiConnect24. "Send it!" does this by default; "Send from PictoCard" still sends from PictoCard's Wii Number. Recipients can be picked from your Wii's address book.
- **Letterheads.** Pick one from a gallery like the one on pictocard.net. The thumbnails are built in, and the letterhead now arrives with letters sent from your Wii.
- **Doodles.** Draw a doodle instead of the PictoCard layout, for email or as a Wii attachment.
- **Message Board pictures.** Browse, preview and send pictures saved on your Wii Message Board.
- **Smooth scrolling.** Hold B and drag to scroll with momentum. Point near the top or bottom edge to scroll, faster the further out you point.
- **Sound effects** for moving the highlight, pressing buttons, and opening and closing lists, from [RiftWii](https://github.com/KakarottoCake/Riftwii).
- A bigger live preview that pages through long letters, livelier confetti, and USB keyboard support throughout.

## Fixes

- Choosing a Message Board picture no longer freezes the Wii.
- The message keyboard stays on screen.
- Brief "secure connection failed" errors are retried automatically.

## Install

Extract PictoCard-Wii.zip to the root of your SD card or USB drive and open PictoCard from the Homebrew Channel.
