## What's new

- **Wii: the system-style keyboard for every field.** Long messages now use it too: it holds up to 2048 characters (libwiiosk 2.2.0) and the text box scrolls as you type.

## Install

Extract the zip for your console to the root of your SD card (Wii also: or USB drive) and open PictoCard from the Homebrew Channel (Wii), the Wii U Menu (Wii U, needs Aroma) or the Homebrew Launcher (3DS).
