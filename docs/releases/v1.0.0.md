The first release of PictoCard for Wii U, ported from PictoCard for Wii 1.1.0.

- Runs from the Wii U Menu with Aroma (`wiiu/apps/PictoCard/PictoCard.wuhb`).
- The GamePad touch screen: tap to select, drag to scroll, draw doodles with your finger or the stylus.
- Take the card picture with the GamePad camera.
- Wii U Miis from Mii Maker, and Wii mode's Miis, Wii Number and address book from the vWii.
- Message Board pictures from Wii mode.
- "Queue in the Wii mode outbox": letters go out from Wii mode's own Wii Number the next time the Wii Menu checks for mail.
